# Cadencea v2 credit policy

Updated 14 October 2026 from the founder's pricing review. This supersedes the earlier demo allowance, rollover and top-up rules.

| Plan | Monthly included credits |
|---|---:|
| Free | 0 |
| Basic | 5 |
| Standard | 10 |
| Pro | 20 |

- No welcome credit grant for new accounts.
- Included credits roll over without a cap. Earned and purchased credits stay available after a downgrade or cancellation.
- Standard purchase value: one credit costs €0.10. Individual audio samples cost one credit; presets cost two.
- Seller earnings remain €0.05 per credit actually spent on their content. Cadencea funds bulk discounts.
- Top-ups: 100 credits for €10, 300 for €28.50 (5% off), and 1,000 for €90 (10% off). Custom top-ups use 100–1,000 credits in steps of 50, with the same discount thresholds.
- The existing 2,000 bought-credit holding limit is separate from rollover; it is retained.
- Refund unspent top-ups within the existing 14-day window using the original discounted purchase rate, never exceeding the amount actually paid.
- Beats remain priced in euros and paid by card.

## Completing a pack

Deduct actual credit spend on valid individual purchases of files included in the same pack version. Count each file once, exclude returned purchases, and never charge less than zero or more than buying the unowned files individually. For a 500-credit pack, 30 credits of qualifying purchases leave 470 credits to pay. Existing pack prices remain seller-selected; no additional automatic pack discount is introduced.

Only the remainder creates a new debit and seller earning. Once a pack is completed, its earlier individual purchases cannot be returned separately while the pack is owned. Returning the pack restores only the completion payment and retains the original individual purchases. Owned files cannot be purchased again.

## Preview implementation

These rules apply to the v2 demo wallet and Marketplace extension previews. They do not introduce live billing, a production wallet, or payouts. Existing demo purchases and historical grants are preserved; a stored wallet adopts the new monthly allowance and unlimited rollover without reseeding its history.

The proposed Cadencea instrument and expansion ecosystem is a separate product idea and has not been added to this implementation.
